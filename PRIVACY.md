# Privacy Policy: Smart URL Copy

Last updated: 2026-10-28

Smart URL Copy copies the title and URL of the current page to your clipboard, in a format you define.

## Data collection

- The extension does not collect personal data.
- The extension does not transmit any data to the developer or to third parties. It makes no network requests.
- The extension does not sell or share data with third parties.
- The extension does not use analytics or tracking.

## Data the extension reads

- The title and URL of the tab you click the toolbar icon on. The extension reads them only at that moment.
- The page title and URL are processed in your browser and written to your clipboard. They are not stored or sent anywhere.

## Data the extension stores

- Your rules, templates, and the active template are saved in `chrome.storage.local`, which lives in your browser profile on your device.
- You can delete them at any time from the options page, or by removing the extension.

## Permissions

- `activeTab`: reads the title and URL of the tab you click.
- `scripting`: runs the copy step on the active page.
- `offscreen`: hosts a hidden document that writes to the clipboard when the page cannot.
- `clipboardWrite`: writes the rendered text to the clipboard.
- `storage`: saves your rules and templates locally.

## Changes to this policy

The developer updates this file when behavior changes. The date above shows the latest update.
